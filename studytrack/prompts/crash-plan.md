# Prompt: 3-Day Emergency Crash Plan Generator
Version: 1.0.0
Author: StudyTrack AI Team

## Purpose
Generates an emergency revision plan prioritized strictly by past-year repeat frequency (Critical topics first) for a student facing an upcoming exam.

## Constraints
1. Allocate maximum time to Critical topics (appeared 4+ of last 5 years).
2. Schedule review slots every 3rd day.
3. Keep daily study load within student specified hours.
